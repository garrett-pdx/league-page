*BPM FANTASY FOOTBALL — WEEK 8 CHECK-IN*
_Monday Nov 2 · written before Bears at Seahawks · six weeks left in the regular season_

Halfway through the regular season, the standings and the scores are telling two different stories. The second-best team in the league is hanging on to the last playoff place on points. The fifteenth-best is holding a playoff place too. And a team that has lost five straight, with a winning record against the whole league, needs two yards from D'Andre Swift tonight to end it.

By Tuesday, seven teams could be 4-4, chasing four playoff places. The table is further down. First, the weekend.

*THE WEEKEND*

*Menisci Barbers of Sevile* posted the week's top score, 139.52, beating It's Shoughtime by 58.92. Tee Higgins 32.50, Brock Bowers 28.20, Jordan Love 27.52, Tony Pollard 26.20. This is a team that opened 0-2, the first of those a 0.18 loss to tvkoesters, and hasn't lost since. Six straight. The only quibble is Kirk Cousins's 34.18 on the bench, and Sleeper had him a point ahead of Love. A week like this can carry that.

*tvkoesters* got the best game anyone played this week: Joe Burrow, 462 yards, five touchdowns, 49.48 points. The other seven starters added 33.30, and *The Himbo Cook Book* won 91.24 to 82.78 behind Patrick Mahomes's 31.44. Emmett Johnson scored 24.80 on the tvkoesters bench, but Sleeper projected him for 3.86 and nobody starts that. This team does not do ordinary weeks. It already owns the second- and third-closest wins in league history and the second-highest losing score, all regular season.

*tichiganben* ended a four-game losing run, 119.50 to 92.56, and *Team substationed* lost with an efficiency of 99.9%. They left 0.10 on the bench and still only beat nine teams' scores. There's no lesson in that, just a fixture list.

*Team Regina Jackson* leads *nyu* 124.58 to 70.20. nyu got 35.60 from Tyler Allgeier and one snap from Lamar Jackson, who hurt his throwing hand on Baltimore's first play. Coach Jesse Minter said he needs surgery and could miss the rest of the season (Yahoo Sports). nyu's only other quarterback is Jacoby Brissett. Smith-Njigba and Loveland need 54.39 tonight; they're projected for 31.27.

*49 IRs* leads *Makai’s Lemonade Stand* 101.18 to 39.00. Makai’s started Michael Penix Jr., who finished on −2.90, over Justin Herbert's 6.78; the projections had Penix ahead, so that one's on Penix. Makai Lemon, for the record, scored 8.70 on The Himbo Cook Book's bench.

*Bridge 4th And Goal*, the defending champion, leads *Avg Mac Jones Enjoyer* 89.40 to 39.18. Avg Mac Jones Enjoyer played the best lineup available. Jake Ferguson played four snaps, and Sleeper now lists an ankle injury. Sometimes it isn't the lineup.

*Are U J(ed Y)orking Me* leads *Can You Diggs It 2.0* 70.30 to 50.20. That 70.30 beats only four scores this week, one of them the opponent's. Can You Diggs It 2.0 started DeVonta Smith, who didn't play (hamstring), and needs 20.11 tonight from Kyle Monangai, whom Sleeper lists as out.

Three gaps stand out.

*Makai’s Lemonade Stand is in a playoff spot with the 15th-best team.* Its all-play record is 35-70, the kind of team that would expect two or three wins by now. It has four. Its last three weeks produced 184.42 points, the fewest in the league, and tonight looks like a fourth loss.

*The Himbo Cook Book is 3-4 with the 2nd-best.* 65-40 in the all-play, and 341.18 points over the last three weeks, more than anyone, yet it holds the last playoff place only on points. This week's win gets it to 4-4, and on points it should be ahead of every 4-4 team.

*49 IRs is 3-4 with the worst all-play record in the league,* 24-81. It's also about to be 4-4, which says more about the schedule than about 49 IRs.

And at the other end: Allen & Allen and Team substationed are both 2-5 with all-play records over .500. Neither has been bad. Both have been unlucky.

*THE ROAD TO DECEMBER*

• *Trade deadline: Week 12.* Four weeks of trading left. It's a keeper league, one keeper a team, so a deal at the deadline can be about next September as much as this December.
• *Six regular-season weeks left* after tonight, Weeks 9 to 14.
• *Eight of sixteen make the playoffs,* seeded by record. Weeks 15, 16 and 17, one week a round.

If tonight goes the way the numbers say, three teams sit at 6-2: Menisci Barbers of Sevile, Team Regina Jackson and Bridge 4th And Goal. Runnin’ while Tuten’ would be 5-3. Then come *seven teams at 4-4* (tvkoesters, Can You Diggs It 2.0, Makai’s Lemonade Stand, The Himbo Cook Book, tichiganben, Are U J(ed Y)orking Me and 49 IRs) for the last four places. Points break the ties. That race is the season.

*TONIGHT — Bears at Seahawks, 5:15 PT, ESPN*

Allen & Allen trails Runnin’ while Tuten’ by 0.18 and needs 0.19 from Swift, projected for 13.14. That is two rushing yards or one catch. If Swift gets there, a five-game losing run ends against the team with the league's best all-play record (81-24) and most points (810.00). If somehow he doesn't, 0.18 ties for the second-closest regular-season finish in league history.

Everything else tonight is arithmetic against long odds.

Five straight losses, and Allen & Allen is one Swift carry from ending it.
