# BPM Fantasy Football — house notes

Read with the `league-monday` skill. This is a starting point assembled in a cloud session on
2026-11-02 from what was visible of the laptop sessions; the fuller rules live in
`~/Desktop/fantasy leagues 2026/CLAUDE.md` on Garrett's laptop and should be merged in here.

## The league

- Sleeper league `1389691887358586880` (2026), history back to 2022 via `previous_league_id`.
- **16 teams, 8 games a week.** Half-PPR, 6-point passing touchdowns, **TE premium (+0.5 a
  catch)**, interceptions −2, fumbles −1 plus −1 more when lost. QB, RB, RB, WR, WR, TE,
  FLEX, FLEX, five bench. Playoffs from week 15, so a 14-week regular season.
- Garrett (Gurret) manages **Runnin’ while Tuten’** — the author has a team in this league.
- Several managers have left since 2022; history shows them by their old handle.

## Rules carried over from the laptop sessions

- **Every team gets a real mention**, not just a table row.
- **Team names, read live every time.** The Week 2 2026 article used three names the teams had
  dropped (one is now "Are U J(ed Y)orking Me"). Sleeper's names use curly apostrophes
  (Runnin’ while Tuten’, Makai’s Lemonade Stand); copy them exactly.
- **Managers are they/them and "the manager"**, never he/him/his. NFL players keep their own.
- **A follow-up starts where the last post stopped.** The post written after the Week 2 Monday
  recap was rebuilt to open with what Monday night changed, cutting everything Monday had
  already told.
- **Fact-check against a fresh pull.** The laptop's `factsheet.py` and this repo's
  `scripts/league-week.py` exist because a draft passed a manual check with errors still in it.
