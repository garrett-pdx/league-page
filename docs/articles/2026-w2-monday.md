## THE RECORD NOBODY WANTS

**Brenden 58.38, Kabroa 48.52. Combined: 106.90.**

In 342 completed matchups since this league moved to Sleeper in 2022, there has never been one this bad. The previous floor was 115.98, a 68.18–47.80 affair between TnT44 and Kabroa in Week 4 of 2023. This game is under it by 9.08 points with one player left.

Brenden's score is final. If it holds up, **58.38 is the lowest winning score in league history** — the old mark was Gurret's 64.12 in Week 14 of last year, and that one at least had the decency to happen in December.

So Isaiah Likely walks into SoFi Stadium tonight carrying two entirely separate jobs. He needs **9.87** to win Kabroa the game. He needs **9.09** to lift this matchup off the bottom of the all-time list. There is a window between those two numbers — nine points and change — where he spares the league the record and loses anyway.

## STILL ALIVE TONIGHT — NYG @ LAR, 8:15

Every unresolved point in this league runs through one football game.

**Kabroa needs 9.87 from Isaiah Likely.** Likely is the Giants' tight end now and the whole record hinges on him. Six catches and seventy yards does it, and he changes two entries in the all-time book.

**kshoyer needs 24.61 from Malik Nabers.** A clean number, because Jonah has nobody left. Nabers is the Giants' primary target and has done it before, but a started receiver in this league has cleared 24.61 in 136 of 2,740 attempts since 2022. That is one week in twenty.

**malstol needs 25.37 from Cam Skattebo, and then some.** paulslaats still has Kyren Williams active in the same game, so every carry Williams takes raises the bar. Skattebo does not merely need a big night — he needs to beat Kyren Williams by 25.37.

Note what that means: **Nabers, Skattebo and Likely are all Giants**, and **Kyren Williams and Davante Adams are both Rams**. Five managers, three live matchups, one game, and the Giants offense has to feed three different men who need 9.87, 24.61, and 25.37-and-climbing. It cannot do all of it. It probably cannot do most of it.
