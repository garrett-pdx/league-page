# THE MUDD REPORT — WEEK 3 PREVIEW

*Thursday Sept 24 · Week 2 in the books · Falcons at Packers tonight, 8:15 · 7 min read*

Monday night made both of Brenden's records permanent, paulslaats paid $29 to rescue a receiver he had cut for nothing three weeks earlier, and two of the league's three unbeaten teams meet this week — one of whom has posted the top score in both weeks.

## MONDAY NIGHT SETTLED IT

Five managers had a starter in Giants at Rams. The three who needed something got almost nothing.

**Tucker got 35.50 from Davante Adams** and went from 104.90 to 140.40 — the top score of the week, again. Monday's edition named jonahcartwright team of the week. Adams took it off him in one evening. Tucker has now posted the league's best score in both weeks of the season.

**Kabroa needed 9.87 from Isaiah Likely.** He got 5.80. Lost 58.38 to 54.32.

**kshoyer needed 24.61 from Malik Nabers.** He got 0.60.

**malstol needed Cam Skattebo to beat Kyren Williams by 25.37.** Skattebo scored 6.50. Williams scored 14.70.

And so both records stand. **58.38 is the lowest winning score in league history**, clear of Gurret's 64.12 from last December. **58.38 to 54.32 — 112.70 combined — is the lowest-scoring matchup** in 342 played since this league moved to Sleeper in 2022. Likely needed 9.09 just to lift the game off the bottom of the list. He did not get there either.

### The cruelest thing that happened to anybody

Kabroa lost to the lowest winning score this league has ever seen, and the one decision still open on Monday night went his way: he started Likely over Dallas Goedert, and Likely outscored him 5.80 to 0.90. It didn't matter.

On his bench sat Chris Godwin, with 7.30. Start Godwin instead of DJ Moore, who went off with an AC sprain for -0.10, and the final reads **61.72 to 58.38**. Kabroa wins.

Nobody would have made that call. Godwin was ranked 103rd in August; Moore was 60th. And the Godwin on that bench was Gurret's eighth-round pick, cut on September 15th and claimed by Kabroa the following day. Gurret's discard would have beaten the lowest winning score of all time, and it spent the afternoon on the bench because Kabroa did the sensible thing.

The only real fight of the week was over a rookie receiver in Cleveland.

Here's why the winning bid is funny. **paulslaats drafted Denzel Boston** — thirteenth round, pick 125 — and **cut him on September 1st**, before he had played a snap. Boston then sat unclaimed for three weeks and caught a touchdown in both games of the season: two catches for 59 and a score in Week 1, five for 95 and a score in Week 2. paulslaats watched all of it and then paid $29 — $18 clear of the next bid — to get back the man he'd given away.

To make room he released Marvin Harrison Jr., his own seventh-round pick. Kabroa took him for $3.

Elsewhere:

**Gurret** moved Jayden Daniels to IR — dislocated elbow, out — and claimed Jared Goff for $3. That is the third time this month Goff has been on Gurret's roster: added September 9th, dropped September 15th, back September 23rd. He also claimed Tyler Shough for nothing and dropped him before the day was out.

**malstol** was the week's other big spender at $27. With Caleb Williams listed doubtful with the hamstring, he cut Jaxson Dart, paid $11 for Bryce Young, and added Tre Tucker ($11) and Adonai Mitchell ($5). Young is his starting quarterback this week.

**kshoyer** took running back Jonah Coleman for $9 over a $0 bid from BBrown16.

In all, $75 changed hands this week, and paulslaats spent $33 of it. He now has $66 left — the least in the league.

## WEEK 3

**The three highest scores in league history were all put up by someone against the manager he plays this week.** Streinz scored 193.04 against TnT44 in 2023. malstol scored 181.26 against Kabroa later that same season. Jonah scored 169.06 against Brenden in 2024. All three pairs meet again on this week's schedule.

### tuckersdumbteam vs paulslaats — the unbeatens

The only meeting of 2-0 teams, and history is lopsided. **Tucker is 5-2 in this matchup**, has outscored paulslaats 731.02 to 691.74, and has won the last three.

Form agrees. Tucker has the top score in both weeks and a 290.16 total nobody is near. paulslaats is coming off 135.88, the second-best score of Week 2, and has just spent a third of his budget on a receiver who keeps scoring touchdowns.

Somebody leaves this 2-1, and it hasn't been Tucker in any of their last three meetings. Tucker also gets a head start tonight: Drake London is in his lineup, and paulslaats has nobody in the game.

### TnT44 vs mikestreinz — the 193

**Streinz is 6-2 in this one and has outscored TnT44 by 214.18.** The single highest score in the history of this league — 193.04, Week 5 of 2023 — was posted against TnT44, who scored 122.52 that day and lost by 70.52.

The standings make it worse. TnT44 is 0-2 with 213.08 points. Streinz is 2-0 with 219.46. Six points and two wins apart. TnT44 scored the third-most points in the league in Week 1 and lost; in Week 2 Josh Allen scored 45.82 and TnT44 lost again. He has managed well, scored well, and has nothing to show for it, and the man across from him owns his worst afternoon.

### Gurret vs kshoyer — the rematch of the third-place game

**kshoyer leads 5-4. Gurret has outscored him anyway, 1030.98 to 1005.64**, and won the most recent meeting — last season's third-place game, 106.46 to 87.38.

kshoyer also holds one of Gurret's worst days. Week 4 of 2023: Gurret scored 140.00 and lost to 160.46. It is still the second-highest losing score in league history.

Neither arrives healthy. Gurret starts Jared Goff at quarterback, which is either full circle or a hostage situation depending on how you count. kshoyer is 0-2, got 0.60 from Nabers on Monday, and has Puka Nacua on IR. He needs this one badly.

### Kabroa vs malstol — the consolation 181

**malstol leads 4-3**, and his 181.26 — the second-highest score in league history — came against Kabroa in Week 16 of 2023. In the **consolation bracket.** The second-best afternoon anyone has ever had in this league was spent in a game for nothing, at Kabroa's expense.

This time the defending champion is 0-2 and starting Bryce Young. Kabroa is 1-1 after losing to the lowest winning score in history, which is its own kind of record. He also starts his week tonight, with Bijan Robinson and Christian Watson on opposite sides of the same field.

### jonahcartwright vs BBrown16 — dead level

**3-3 in six meetings**, and Jonah has outscored Brenden by 12.68 across them. Jonah's 169.06, the third-highest score in league history, came against Brenden in Week 15 of 2024.

Jonah is 1-1 with the second-most points in the league. Brenden is 1-1 with the fewest and a win that is now in the record book for all the wrong reasons.

As of Thursday afternoon, **Patrick Mahomes is in Brenden's starting lineup.** After 25.66 and 34.98 from the bench, third time lucky.
