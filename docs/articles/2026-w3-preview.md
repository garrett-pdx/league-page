# THE MUDD REPORT — WEEK 3 PREVIEW

*Thursday Sept 24 · Week 2 in the books · Falcons at Packers tonight, 8:15 · 4 min read*

## MONDAY NIGHT SETTLED IT

**Tucker got 35.50 from Davante Adams** and jumped from 104.90 to 140.40 — the top score of the week, again. Monday's edition named Jonah team of the week. Adams took it off him in one evening.

The three managers who needed something got almost nothing. Kabroa needed 9.87 from Isaiah Likely and got 5.80. kshoyer needed 24.61 from Malik Nabers and got 0.60. malstol needed Cam Skattebo to beat Kyren Williams by 25.37; it went 6.50 to 14.70.

So both records stand. **58.38 is the lowest winning score in league history**, and **58.38 to 54.32 is the lowest-scoring matchup** of the 342 played since 2022.

### The cruelest thing that happened to anybody

Kabroa lost to the lowest winning score ever, and even the one call still open on Monday went his way: Likely outscored Dallas Goedert 5.80 to 0.90.

On his bench sat Chris Godwin, with 7.30. Start him over DJ Moore, who left with an AC sprain for -0.10, and Kabroa wins **61.72 to 58.38**. Nobody would have — Godwin was ranked 103rd in August, Moore 60th. And that Godwin was Gurret's eighth-round pick, cut September 15th and claimed by Kabroa the next day. Gurret's discard would have beaten the lowest winning score of all time, and it sat on the bench because Kabroa did the sensible thing.
