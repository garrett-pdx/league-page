# The 2026 draft, and where it leaves everyone

_Derived 2026-09-08 by `scripts/derive-draft-report.py`. Every number here is computed; see `static/data/draft-2026.json` for the facts block._

## Method

- Picks graded against the market **as it stood the day before the draft** (2026-08-22T13:37:32.704Z), not today's.
- Rosters ranked on **live** cardinal values, best legal starting eight (QB/RB/RB/WR/WR/TE/FLEX/FLEX).
- Graded 109 of 110 live picks; 91 of 130 also carry real-league ADP (that feed excludes QB).

Graded **within position**: an overall trade-value rank against a pick number is
meaningless in a one-QB league, where quarterbacks are cheap to trade for and still
have to be drafted by all ten teams.
