<p class="homeLede">Ten teams. Half-PPR. Two keepers each, and a long memory.</p>

This is a demo of **[Fantasy Football Website with History](https://github.com/garrett-pdx/fantasy-football-website-with-history)**, running on a real Sleeper league. The standings, matchups and rosters are live; every past season, record, rivalry and Trophy Room entry was built from the league's Sleeper history by the data pipeline. Point it at your own league and this page becomes yours.
