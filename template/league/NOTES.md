# The Demo League: notes for agents

This is the template's demo league: a real Sleeper league with neutral content (handles instead
of names, no photos, no bios), so the template shows every page with real data. Replace all of
`league/` with your own league's; the `league-setup` skill does it.

- The data is a real keeper league: ten teams, half-PPR, two keepers, Sleeper seasons from 2022,
  and a founding 2021 season on ESPN whose draft was imported into Sleeper
  (`history.preSleeper`).
- `overrides` pins two post-settlement stat corrections and the positions Sleeper allowed two
  players, so every manager-season reproduces Sleeper's potential points exactly.
