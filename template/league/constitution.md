A sample constitution, drafted from the league's Sleeper settings. Each `##` heading in
`league/constitution.md` is a numbered section and each `###` a numbered rule.

## The League

### Format

A ten-team keeper league. Every season the whole player pool returns to the draft except the players each manager keeps, and keepers cost draft picks (see Section 4).

The regular season runs Weeks 1 through 15; the postseason runs Weeks 16 and 17.

## Rosters and Lineups

### Starting Lineup

Each team starts eight players every week:

- 1 &times; QB
- 2 &times; RB
- 2 &times; WR
- 1 &times; TE
- 2 &times; FLEX (RB, WR or TE)

Lineups lock at each player's kickoff.

### Bench and Injured Reserve

Five bench spots and two Injured Reserve slots, which do not count against the roster limit.

## Scoring

### Scoring Values

Half-PPR with six-point passing touchdowns: a point per 25 passing yards, a point per 10 rushing or receiving yards, six points per touchdown, half a point per reception, minus two per interception and minus one per fumble lost.

## Keepers

### How Many

Each team may keep up to two players from last season's roster.

### Keeper Cost

A keeper costs the round he was drafted last season, and one round earlier each further season the same manager keeps him. A player who went undrafted costs a final-round pick.

## Waivers and Trades

### FAAB

Waivers run on a free agent budget of 100 dollars a season.

### Trade Deadline

No trades after Week 12.

## Postseason

### Playoffs

Four teams make the playoffs: semifinals in Week 16, the final and third-place game in Week 17. A consolation bracket decides fifth through eighth.
