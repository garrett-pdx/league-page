# THE MUDD REPORT — WEEK 6 PREVIEW

*Thursday Oct 15 · Week 5 in the books · Seahawks at Broncos under way, 5:15 PT · 4 min read*

## THE QUARTERBACK CAROUSEL

Six quarterbacks were cut or claimed in this league between Tuesday evening and Thursday kickoff. Two of them had just scored 33.94 and 28.00.

**Gurret cut Jared Goff on Tuesday.** Goff started all four weeks he spent on Gurret's roster this season and scored 19.44, 23.36, 22.48 and 33.94. Sleeper had projected him as the No. 1 quarterback in football last week, and he finished as Gurret's best player. Detroit is on bye this week and Jayden Daniels is healthy, so the move has a logic to it. It also has a punchline: this is the second time Gurret has cut Goff this season, and nobody put in a claim. The No. 1 projected quarterback of Week 5 is a free agent.

**Tucker cut Aaron Rodgers on Thursday afternoon**, one start and 28.00 points after picking him up. Lamar Jackson is back in Tucker's lineup, listed questionable with a sprained ankle after missing practice again on Thursday ([Newsweek](https://www.newsweek.com/sports/nfl/ravens-insider-says-lamar-jacksons-week-6-injury-status-seems-pretty-obvious-12575217)). Tucker's backup is now Bryce Young, whom malstol paid **$11** for in Week 2, started twice, and cut during his bye.

**Kabroa cut Bo Nix**, his 11th-round pick, who plays for Denver tonight as a free agent. Nix's replacement drew the week's only three-way bidding war:

| Manager | Jordan Love | Result |

Gurret, having released the projected No. 1 quarterback, then bid nothing for Jordan Love on two separate claims and lost both. He also bid nothing for Germie Bernard, the Steelers receiver, and lost that to kshoyer's **$6**.

**kshoyer** has been the busiest manager since Monday. He paid **$7** for Dameon Pierce on Wednesday and cut him on Thursday afternoon for Kirk Cousins. Joe Burrow is on bye, and Jalen Hurts is out with a blood clot in his calf ([NFL.com](https://www.nfl.com/news/eagles-qb-jalen-hurts-out-sunday-panthers-superficial-blood-clot)), so for the first time this season kshoyer has no Burrow-or-Hurts decision to make. He starts Cousins.

## WEEK 6

### paulslaats vs jonahcartwright — the rivalry

Designated rivals, and the series has a title in it. paulslaats leads 6-3 and has outscored Jonah by 113.84. The ladder of margins, from paulslaats's side: −21.00, +15.12, **+31.80**, +22.10, +13.62, +3.44, −1.76, **+60.42**, −9.90.

The +31.80 was the **2022 championship game**, 76.68 to 44.88. Jonah's 44.88 is still the third-lowest score in league history, and it came in the one game he most needed. paulslaats has led the series ever since. The +60.42 was 2025's Week 11, 139.08 to 78.66, the biggest score either has posted against the other. Jonah won the most recent meeting, a 2025 seventh-place game, 70.68 to 60.78, which is the kind of revenge that does not get a banner.

Now paulslaats is 4-1 with the most points in the league, and Jonah is 2-3.

### tuckersdumbteam vs Gurret — the only 6-0

Only one team in league history has started 6-0: Gurret, in 2022. This week he plays the team trying to join him. Gurret leads the series 4-3, by 10.56 points, and two meetings were decided by less than a point. Gurret took the 2022 third-place game 111.30 to 110.62; Tucker took the last meeting, in 2025, 109.36 to 108.54. Tucker is 5-0 and has a questionable quarterback in his lineup. Gurret starts Daniels, without the safety net he cut on Tuesday.

### TnT44 vs Kabroa — somebody has to win

TnT44 is 0-5. Two teams in league history have started 0-5 and both won in Week 6. Nobody has ever started 0-6. Kabroa is 1-4, and on Monday he became the first manager in league history to lose four straight games his bench would have won. Kabroa leads the series 4-3, TnT44 has outscored him by 11.32, and their last meeting was the second-closest finish in league history: 95.80 to 95.58 to Kabroa, in Week 15 of 2025. One of them is about to feel much better.

### mikestreinz vs malstol — 4-4

Level at four wins each, Streinz ahead by 47.74 points. The low point belongs to malstol: 51.54 in Week 15 of 2024, a 56.86-point loss to Streinz. Streinz has Trevor Lawrence in his lineup at quarterback this week. malstol starts Jacoby Brissett again, and the defending champion has had 613.44 points scored against him, the second-most in the league.

### kshoyer vs BBrown16 — both on Thursday

kshoyer leads 4-2 and has outscored Brenden by 54.36. Brenden has won two of the last three. kshoyer starts Cousins; Brenden starts Matthew Stafford, his Week 5 pickup.
