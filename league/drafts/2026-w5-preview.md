## BRENDEN OPENS THE SHOP

Two trades went through in Week 4, and this publication has mentioned neither. Both were with Brenden.

| Brenden sent | Brenden got |
| --- | --- |
| Justin Jefferson (his 1st-rounder) | Stefon Diggs |
| Colston Loveland (his 3rd) | Denzel Boston |
| Nico Collins (his keeper) | Gurret's 2027 3rd |
| 2027 7th and 8th | paulslaats's 2027 3rd |
| 2028 12th | paulslaats's 2028 9th |

Jefferson and a 2027 seventh went to Gurret for Diggs and Gurret's third. Loveland, Collins and two late picks went to paulslaats for Boston, a third and a ninth. In one week a 1-2 team with the fewest points in the league turned its first-round pick, its third-round pick and its keeper into two receivers and two 2027 thirds.

The timing was cruel. The paulslaats deal went through on Saturday. On Sunday Collins scored **27.30** for his new manager, inside the 170.12. Brenden lost to Gurret by 10.38, with Courtland Sutton (0.90), Jauan Jennings (1.30) and Emeka Egbuka (2.30) in his receiver slots. His lineup was 100% efficient. It just didn't have Nico Collins in it anymore.

There is a longer game here. Brenden has kept Collins two years running, at a twelfth-round price this year, and under the constitution the inflation follows the manager, not the player: paulslaats can keep him next year for a twelfth. Brenden would have paid an eleventh. Gurret's Jefferson costs a first.

Boston, for his part, has a new home with the one manager who bid **$0** for him in Week 2. Brenden starts him this week, which is one more start than he got in two weeks on paulslaats's bench.

## WEEK 5

### TnT44 vs kshoyer — the series nobody has won

Six meetings. kshoyer has won all six. No one else in league history has played another manager four times without beating him. The closest TnT44 has come was 86.50 to 83.40 in 2023, and he has been outscored 643.80 to 533.80 overall. kshoyer has one win this season, from Monday. TnT44 has none, and draws the one opponent he has never beaten.

### paulslaats vs Kabroa — two in a row

paulslaats leads 6-2 and has won the last two, 94.52 to 89.70 and 122.44 to 80.50, both in 2025. He is second in points, has Collins in his lineup and two Cowboys starting tonight. Kabroa lost Monday by 10.06 to the unbeaten team and has nobody playing until Sunday. A loss puts him at 1-4.

### mikestreinz vs BBrown16 — 44.52

Brenden leads 3-2 and has outscored Streinz by 42.04. Two of their meetings matter. In Week 11 of 2025 Streinz scored **44.52** against him, still the second-lowest score in league history. In Week 17 of 2025 they played for fifth place, and Brenden won it 96.04 to 94.88. Streinz's unbeaten start ended Monday; Brenden's rebuilt receiver room gets its first week together.

### tuckersdumbteam vs jonahcartwright — the semifinal

Tucker leads 4-3, but the meeting that counted was Jonah's: the 2022 semifinal, 124.20 to 108.38, the one that sent Jonah to the final. Tucker is 4-0 and leads the league in points; Jonah is 2-2 and lost by 45.16 last week. Jonah starts Dak Prescott and Bucky Irving tonight. Tucker starts the running back Jonah cut.

### Gurret vs malstol — the one that counted

Gurret is 6-3 and has outscored malstol by **161.14** across nine meetings. malstol won the one that mattered: the 2025 semifinal, 129.06 to 125.98, on the way to the title. The defending champion is 2-2 and third in points. Gurret is 3-1, starts George Pickens tonight, and starts Justin Jefferson for the first time.
