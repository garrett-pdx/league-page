# THE MUDD REPORT — WEEK 7 PREVIEW

*Thursday Oct 22 · Week 6 in the books · Patriots at Bears tonight, 5:15 PT · 4 min read*

## THE BYE-WEEK AUDIT

Buffalo, Jacksonville, the Chargers and Washington are all off this week, and as of Thursday afternoon the league's lineups had not entirely noticed.

**Gurret has one quarterback on his roster.** It is Jayden Daniels, he plays for Washington, and he is in Gurret's starting lineup for a week Washington does not play. Gurret has cut Jared Goff twice this season, most recently on October 13. Goff is still a free agent. So are Aaron Rodgers, Jordan Love, Sam Darnold, Bo Nix and Jacoby Brissett, every one of them cut by someone in this league since the start of Week 5. The carousel has stopped, and everybody got off at the waiver wire.

**kshoyer is starting Dalton Kincaid**, who plays for Buffalo, and Buffalo is on bye. James Cook, also a Bill, has been moved out of the lineup. Kincaid was missed.

**Brenden has an empty running back slot.** He claimed Kendre Miller for **$2** on Wednesday and has not put him in it.

**TnT44 is starting Jordan Mason at flex.** Mason is on injured reserve.

Lineups lock at each player's kickoff, so all of this can be fixed by Sunday. This publication will be checking.

## THE WAIVER WIRE

**Kabroa** is on his third quarterback in three weeks. Nix went first, for Jordan Love. Love started once and scored 22.24. Kabroa cut him on Wednesday, along with Darnold, and picked up C.J. Stroud, who had just thrown for 312 yards and three touchdowns. Love cost **$3**. Stroud cost nothing.

**Jonah** spent his first FAAB dollars of the season: **$5** on Omar Cooper Jr., the Jets rookie who caught six passes for 103 yards and a touchdown on Sunday.

**Brenden** cut Terry McLaurin, his sixth-round pick, after McLaurin's one-catch, 27-yard Monday night. He also bid nothing for Tyrone Tracy and didn't get him.

**TnT44** picked up Kyler Murray, with Josh Allen on bye, and cut Kenyon Sadiq to make room. The rookie tight end who once outscored a starter from TnT44's bench, and was then started for zero, is a free agent.
