### Gurret vs mikestreinz — the rivalry

The rivalry belongs to Streinz, 5-4, with an 86.76-point edge. He went ahead in 2023 with 162.48 and 168.12 in back-to-back meetings, the second of them still the fifth-highest score in league history, and has led ever since, apart from a 4-4 tie after Week 12 last season. He also won their only postseason meeting, a 2024 consolation semifinal, 126.66 to 102.42. Week 2 this season went to Streinz by 1.82. Sleeper projects Gurret by 25.45. Streinz starts Jonathan Taylor tonight and, as of this afternoon, nobody at tight end on Sunday.

### paulslaats vs malstol

Level at 4-4, with paulslaats ahead by 51.54 in points. He won Week 2, 135.88 to 102.32; malstol won the last meeting of 2025, 124.28 to 78.32. paulslaats is the first team in five Sleeper seasons to go 9-1, and four of his six reserves are on bye. malstol has Caleb Williams back, Jaxon Smith-Njigba on bye, and Cam Skattebo, RB9, in the lineup this time.

### kshoyer vs jonahcartwright

kshoyer leads 6-2 and has outscored Jonah by 88.38. He won six straight meetings from 2023 to 2025; Jonah ended that in Week 2, 130.96 to 106.96. kshoyer's lineup is projected for 122.37, the most of anyone this week, behind Joe Burrow, QB1. Puka Nacua is on IR, Josh Jacobs is listed out, and Wan'Dale Robinson, last week's $6 claim, is now listed on injured reserve. Jonah gets Bucky Irving and Luther Burden III back from their byes.

### BBrown16 vs Kabroa — the rivalry

Brenden leads 6-2. Kabroa has outscored him by 4.50. That is the series: Brenden won 99.70 to 98.96 in 2024, among the five closest finishes in league history, and then beat Kabroa in the 2024 championship game, 153.86 to 138.56, still the third-highest losing score on record. Their Week 2 meeting this season, 58.38 to 54.32, has the lowest combined score of any game on record. Sleeper projects Kabroa by 27.01. Brenden starts Austin Ekeler and Devin Singletary, RB50 and RB51, because with De'Von Achane on IR and Jadarian Price on bye there is no one else. His 18 pickups are the second-most in the league. Kabroa starts two tight ends, and every player on his bench is on bye or listed out, so for one week his bench cannot beat him.
